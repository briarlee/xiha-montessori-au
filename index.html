Questions About Ordering in Australia

Yes, we provide door-to-door delivery to all Australian states and territories including NSW, VIC, QLD, WA, SA, TAS, ACT and the NT. Our logistics partners handle customs, port clearance, and last-mile delivery to your centre's door.
Our furniture is designed to comply with AS/NZS 4610:2020 (educational furniture), AS 4684-2009 (highchairs), and aligns with the National Quality Standard (NQS) Quality Areas 2 and 3. All materials are non-toxic, lead-free, and use food-grade finishes. We can provide relevant compliance documentation upon request.
Absolutely! We offer a free classroom design service. Simply send us your floor plan and room dimensions, and our design team will create a professional 2D/3D layout showing furniture placement, learning zones, and traffic flow — tailored to your age group and educational approach (Montessori, Reggio, or Waldorf).
Standard production takes 15–30 days depending on the order size and customisation level. Shipping to Australian ports typically takes 14–21 days. For urgent orders, we can arrange expedited production and air freight. We recommend allowing 6–8 weeks total for planning purposes.
